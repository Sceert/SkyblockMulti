# SkyblockMulti Roadmap

This roadmap describes development direction, not guaranteed release dates.

## 0.1.x — Multiplayer Foundation

Status: **Completed checkpoint (`v0.1.1-beta`)**

- Island allocation
- Circular 8/16/24 geometry
- Tree selection
- Player difficulty selection
- Persistent world geometry
- Optional OpenPAC integration
- Party lifecycle
- Respawn handling
- Lore book
- Secret End advancement

## 0.2.x — World Foundation

Status: **Current development**

- Last Refuge HUB
- Protected glass dome
- Natural terrain inside the refuge
- Transparent observation floor
- Fortress beneath the HUB
- Stronghold-style End portal chamber
- 0% / 25% / 75% initial End Portal Eye configuration
- Per-world structural persistence
- No direct dome-to-fortress access
- Foundation for later dungeon/progression access

## Later development

Potential future phases include:

- Lateral dungeon/access route to the central fortress
- Broader structured progression
- Review/adaptation of vanilla advancements for Skyblock
- Additional procedural exploration content
- Flooded ancient-world dimension concept
- Selected structures required for otherwise unavailable progression
- Further multiplayer balancing and optimization

These items remain subject to testing and design changes.
